## 5. Coverage & Negative Results (8 Tracks)

The following controls were explicitly audited across snapshots `bedbe50` / post-remediation and verified as holding with no bypasses found during review:

*   **Track 1 & 2 (Core Invariants, Airlock & Gateway Runtime):**
    *   Strict 1-way dependency DAG (`mellyn.core` stdlib-only core + lazy Pydantic; `mellyn.egress` -> `mellyn.core`; `mellyn.airlock` -> `mellyn.core` + `mellyn.egress`; `mellyn.ir_lab` -> `mellyn.core`) enforced programmatically in `tests/unit/test_core_normalize_and_schemas.py::TestDependencyDag::test_strict_one_way_dag`.
    *   Strict wire models across `core` and `airlock` (`ConfigDict(extra="forbid", strict=True, frozen=True)`), duplicate JSON key rejection, and fail-closed `ZoneEvent` validation (`verdict_allow` consistency).
    *   Subprocess environment isolation (minimal explicit `env` dict, secret-env filtering from downstream child processes) and Airlock `stdout` wire purity (diagnostics strictly confined to `stderr` / audit ledger).
    *   AgBOM exact-byte pinning (`exact_json_bytes`) rejects missing pins, runtime tool-definition mutations (rug pulls), and invisible Unicode tag injection.
*   **Track 4 & 5 (Scanner & Forensic Ledger):**
    *   Audit ledger hashes raw unredacted bytes (`artifact_sha256`) prior to scrubbing secrets/canaries, writes strictly sequential hash chains (`sha256_canonical`, `seq`, `prev_hash`, `entry_hash`), and detects single-byte tampering, line deletions, reorders, and injections via `mellyn-ir` (`mellyn.ir_lab`).
*   **Track 7 & 8 (Deployment, Containers & CI Guardrails):**
    *   `Dockerfile.airlock`, `Dockerfile.gateway`, `Dockerfile.authorizer`, `Dockerfile.egress`, and `compose.yaml` isolate audit/state volumes and pin pip installs with hashes.
    *   `.github/workflows/ai-security-gate.yml` pins `mellyn-guardrails` by commit SHA, preserves required wrapper job names matching branch rulesets, and enforces Ruff baseline rules without `per-file-ignores` suppression (`tests/test_pr7_ci_gate_and_egress_probe.py`).
